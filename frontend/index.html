Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Ngaturin

What is Ngaturin? ▼
Ngaturin is a financial management platform designed to make personal finance fun and accessible for young adults. We combine AI-powered coaching with goal-oriented savings tracking to help you take control of your money.
How does the AI Coach work? ▼
Our AI Coach is your personal finance buddy! Ask questions about budgeting, saving, or managing money, and get instant, easy-to-understand answers. It's like having a financial advisor available 24/7.
What is Goal Saver? ▼
Goal Saver helps you set specific financial goals (like buying a new phone or saving for a trip) and shows you exactly how much you need to save daily or monthly. You can track your progress and stay motivated as you watch your savings grow!
Is Ngaturin free to use? ▼
Yes! Ngaturin offers a free tier with access to core features including the AI Coach and Goal Saver. Premium features may be added in the future.
Is my financial data secure? ▼
Absolutely! We take security seriously. All your data is encrypted and stored securely. We never share your personal information with third parties without your consent.
How do I get started? ▼
Simply click the "Sign In" button to create your free account. Once registered, you'll be able to access both the AI Coach and Goal Saver features immediately!
